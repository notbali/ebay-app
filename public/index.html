Nothing Sails Without Your Say-SoAI drafts, you decide

AI is fast at drafting, but pricing, condition, and category are judgment calls — not something to publish blind. The Bali Bay is built around one rule: nothing goes live on eBay without you explicitly saying so.

  • Draft-only, always. Every AI-generated listing lands as an unpublished draft. Publishing is a separate, deliberate step — yours.
  • Your eBay account, not ours. You connect your own seller account. Everything you publish goes live under your store.
  • Edit everything. Title, price, description, condition, specifics — all editable before a draft ever reaches eBay.

Built for the Parts You Actually SellNot a generic listing tool

Industrial and electrical parts don't list themselves the way a used paperback does — illegible labels, obscure part numbers, and specs that matter to the right buyer. The Bali Bay is tuned for exactly that.
